About Me

Hi! I'm Rasmus -- a software consultant based in Barcelona with over 14 years of professional experience, currently working at Clovr Labs. I'm a system architect at heart who equally loves the creative side of building products -- from designing robust backends to crafting the interactions that make software feel alive.

I work across the full stack with Python, Go, Rust, and JavaScript as my primary tools. I'm deeply into AI/ML and agentic systems, and I'm always exploring new ways to turn ideas into shipped products.

I speak Danish natively, English fluently, and I'm picking up Spanish along the way. When I'm not coding, you'll find me being a dad, exploring Barcelona, tinkering with side projects, or diving into whatever catches my curiosity.
