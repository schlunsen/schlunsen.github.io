# Rasmus Schlünsen: career reel, painted (60 s)

Logline: fifteen years of building, told as a table of painted objects that assemble, click, spin and multiply,
one chapter per stage of the career.

World: warm paper (#F3EBDC) under everything, ink #2B2233 outlines, flat washes in the site palette (clay, ochre, teal,
sap, indigo, violet, rose, sky, cream). Each chapter owns one accent colour; the wipe into a chapter is painted in it.
Colour arc: warm clay (open) → cold indigo (security) → violet/sky (Unity) → teal (agency) → sap/sky (wind) →
ochre/clay (money) → indigo/teal (n0) → the full palette on the board (workshop) → warm clay again (end).

Motif: the site's orbit logo (a circle with two tilted ellipses and a dot). It is drawn on in the open around a tower
of blocks and returns, fully painted, on the end card. Blocks (small painted modules) recur: they build the tower,
become the Unity cube, the browser/API/DB boxes and the workspace tiles.

Lettering: Permanent Marker, one short headline per chapter (plus the name on the end card and the project names
on the workshop cards). Headlines pop in with overshoot and a painted underline swash draws on under them.

Flicker rule: boil reseeds 4×/s (not 12), jitter amplitudes ≤ 3 px, backgrounds and watercolour fills use a
still seed (no boil) or are baked once, so only object linework breathes.

Transitions: a brush wipe at every chapter seam (0.3 s cover + 0.3 s drag-off, cut under full cover), painted in
the colours of the incoming chapter. The film opens by dragging a clay wipe off the first frame and ends by painting
the same clay wipe back on, so it loops cleanly on the site.
